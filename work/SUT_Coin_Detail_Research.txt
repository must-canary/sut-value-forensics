SUT COIN - RESEARCH NOTE
SuperTrust (SUT)
Researched 29 Sep 2026
Not financial or legal advice. Crypto prices change by the minute, and the figures below are a snapshot from the
sources listed at the end, taken on 29 Sep 2026. Allegations reported in the media are marked "REPORTED"; they are
not proven.
v2 CORRECTS v1 ON THREE POINTS
1. The March 2026 BitMart delisting and the April 2026 "contract swap" were NOT SuperTrust. They were a different
token, Sanity United, which also used the ticker SUT. CoinMarketCap's AI news page mixed the two up, and v1 repeated
the error.
2. The "issuer can freeze sells / mint tokens" warning is out of date. The verified contract cannot mint and has no fees or
blacklist. Its only admin power was pausing transfers, and ownership was given up (renounced) on 20 Jul 2026, so
nobody can pause it now.
3. CertiK's own site scores SUT 74.06 (grade BBB), not the "3.7" that CoinMarketCap displays.
0. WHICH "SUT"?
At least three unrelated tokens have used the ticker SUT:
SuperTrust (SUT) — This note. Polygon contract 0x98965474ecbec2f532f1f780ee37b0b05f77ca55
Super Useless Token — A small game token for the "MORCHI" mini-game, also on Polygon.
Sanity United (SUT) — An Ethereum token that BitMart listed and delisted in 2026. It has since been swapped into a
new token called SU.
Always check the contract address.
1. IN ONE PARAGRAPH
SuperTrust (SUT) is a Polygon token launched on 29 Jan 2024, issued by MUST COMPANY SG PTE. LTD. (Singapore) and
run in Korea by 주식회사 슈퍼트러스트 (SUPERTRUST Co., Ltd.), with CEO Kim Hak-eung. It is marketed as a payment token
for a group of platforms (ads, film, travel, nature mapping) that give discounts when you pay in SUT. The code is a simple,
verified token with ownership now given up. But the market for it is extremely thin, a few wallets hold most of the supply,
and Korean media have linked it to MSQUARE GLOBAL's "SuperSave" scheme: a reported criminal complaint alleging fraud
and illegal fund-raising, a bank freezing its accounts, and a Korean exchange (GOPAX) delisting it. The price is about $0.40,
97% below its September 2025 peak.

3. LINK TO MSQUARE / MSQ
The evidence points to the same group, although the company now denies it.
Nate/news (8 Jan 2025) headline: "엠스퀘어㈜ 계열사 ㈜슈퍼트러스트", i.e. SuperTrust is an affiliate of MSQUARE.
천지일보 (23 Dec 2024) reported an MSQUARE briefing that described it as issuing "MSQ, SUT" and paying returns
through "SuperSave". Korea's Press Arbitration Commission later ordered a correction: MSQUARE did not promise a
fixed 140% return.
Fieldnews (14 Nov 2025): "MSQUARE GLOBAL and affiliate SuperTrust" filed a joint defamation complaint.
The supertrust.club site code uses labels like msquare_nature_book, msquare_l2u and msquare_zeroplus.
Nature Book pays out in P2U coins, and P2U runs on the MSQUARE MARKET app.
Company position (8 Aug 2026): SuperTrust and MSQUARE GLOBAL are "separate businesses, operated
independently" (supertrust.club notice 4615).
Nexus (our QA agent) is currently reviewing the msquare-admin-frontend repository.

11. RISKS AND PROBLEMS
11a. Legal and reputational (most serious)
1. REPORTED criminal complaint.
A reporter filed a fraud and illegal fund-raising (유사수신) complaint with the Seoul police against MSQUARE GLOBAL and
affiliates including SuperTrust. Allegations:
30,000+ investors recruited through the unlicensed "SuperSave" platform;
a pitch of "10M KRW -> 24M KRW in a year" using SUT;
a reported investor death, which SuperTrust calls false.
The outcome is unknown. Source: smartfn, seen via search snippet only.
2. Bank account freeze (verified).
Shinhan Bank froze SuperTrust's deposits in Apr 2025, treating it as an unregistered virtual-asset business. A court upheld
the freeze; the company appealed.
3. GOPAX delisting (Aug-Sep 2025).
The exchange cited possible legal violations and non-disclosure, per search snippets.
4. REPORTED Ponzi concerns.
A lawyer's public post calls the scheme a "typical Ponzi suspicion" (opinion). SuperTrust's own notices show buyback and
"credit" offers tied to joining SuperSave, and a stated plan for a "short-term SUT value surge".
5. Legal action against critics.
SuperTrust and MSQUARE sued the operator of a victims' chat room for defamation (sent to prosecutors) and filed a ~KRW
2B damages claim.
6. A conflict of interest for us.
The issuer's name and Singapore address appear on MUST Company's website, and Nexus is reviewing MSQUARE code.
11b. Market
7. Very little trading: ~$70K a day against a ~$75M valuation.
Order books are ~$4K-$12K deep, so prices are easy to move and exiting a position may be hard.

12. Concentrated holdings: the top 10 wallets hold ~71% (~50% excluding the dead address).
One unlabelled wallet holds 21%.
13. The liquidity pool is unlocked and 4 wallets control it.
14. Supply figures conflict (188.4M, 46.6M or 2.02M "circulating"), and GOPAX's 47.8% foundation
holding contradicts the "zero reserve" claim.
15. The 50M "burn" is a transfer to a dead address, not a reduction in total supply.
16. No security audit, no team identity check (KYC), and no bug bounty.
(Improvement: ownership has been renounced, so the old pause risk is gone.)
11d. Project and utility
17. Most platforms are unproven: 50+ downloads for ZERO PLUS, no product found for AI Studio 48
or SUT GOLD Connect, and L2U and NATURUBOOK run by other companies.
18. The 30-50% fee discount, SUT's main selling point, applies only to South Korean users of the
operator's own platforms.
19. The advisors the project claims (TON Network, Outlier Ventures) can't be verified.
20. Information is unreliable: CoinMarketCap's AI summaries mixed SUT up with another token,
and data sites disagree on price records, supply and rank.
