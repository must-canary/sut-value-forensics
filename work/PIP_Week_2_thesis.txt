2. WHY SUT SITS AROUND #300
1. The rank comes from an accounting change.
CoinMarketCap started counting 188.4M SUT as circulating (instead of 2.02M) on
10 Feb 2026. SUT went from #2,049 to #299 overnight at the same price. It was
not growth.

2. Almost nobody trades it.
About $70K a day, mostly on one exchange (Gate). Its market cap is not backed
by real buying and selling.
3. Trust problems.
Reported fraud allegations, a bank freeze, a GOPAX delisting, three unexplained
crashes, and ~71% of tokens in 10 wallets.
4. Usage is unproven.
Most platforms have little activity, and the main benefit is a discount for Korean
users only.

5. POSSIBILITIES - CAN SUT REACH THE
TOP 100?
Mathematically: yes. A price of about $2.19 (with 188.4M circulating) would reach
the ~$413M threshold. SUT traded above that for most of 2025.
Realistically: not soon, and not the way it is framed. Two different routes lead
there, and only one is sound.
[NOT SOUND] Pushing the price or rank
Using volume, buybacks or supply-figure changes.
With markets this thin, the price can be pushed up cheaply, but it collapses
when support stops. That is what the three past crashes look like.
It is also market manipulation, which carries legal risk for the people involved.
[SOUND] Earning it, the way RWA and payment peers did
1. Fix trust first: register with regulators, resolve the bank and legal issues,
publish a wallet map and one supply figure, and lock the liquidity.
2. Prove it is safe: an independent smart-contract audit, a team KYC and a bug
bounty.
3. Prove it is used: publish real SoloPay and platform payment volume and active
users, ideally checkable on-chain.
4. If "RWA" is the story: back SUT with audited, redeemable assets and regular
reserve reports, like PAX Gold and Tether Gold.
