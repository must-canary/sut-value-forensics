1. THE TASK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From the CTO:
  "The SUT project is currently ranked within the top 500 in the crypto
   market, which is a solid baseline. As a real-world asset (RWA) project, if
   we properly verify and prove our value, breaking into the top 100 is well
   within reach. Built on the Solo Unicorn framework and backed by the
   SoloPay crypto payment system, let's focus on validating this thesis
   further this week."

How QA reads it
  - "Verify and prove our value" is quality work: test the product, check the
    data, collect evidence.
  - "Validate the thesis" means testing whether "SUT can reach the top 100"
    holds up against evidence. That covers what supports it, what blocks it,
    and what would have to be true.
  - Deadline: 2 Oct 2026. No further clarification is available, so this
    plan sets its own scope.


2. THE DELIVERABLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"SUT Top-100 Thesis Validation Report" (QA), handed in on 2 Oct 2026.

  - What supports the thesis   evidence verified by testing or on-chain
  - What blocks it             gaps and risks, each with its source
  - What would have to be true measurable top-100 targets and a roadmap

Every statement in the report is either tested by QA or backed by a cited
source. Anything that can't be verified is marked "unverified".

6. WORKING PRINCIPLES (what QA will and won't do)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Will do
    - Test, verify and report facts, with evidence and sources.
    - Recommend legitimate ways to improve: audits, correct data, product
      quality, real usage, transparency.
    - Mark anything unverified clearly.

  Will not do
    - Any trading, liquidity, volume or price activity, or anything aimed at
      moving the rank directly.
    - Change or "adjust" supply figures to affect the rank. Only correct them
      to the documented truth.
    - Publish marketing claims ("top 100", "RWA") that aren't backed by
      evidence.
    - Price predictions.

  Record keeping
    - Keep the original task text, this plan, test evidence and the final
      report together.
    - Write findings in neutral, factual language, with sources.
