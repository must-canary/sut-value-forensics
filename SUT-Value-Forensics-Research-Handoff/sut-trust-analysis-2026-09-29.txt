SUT (SUPERTRUST) - USER TRUST ANALYSIS
Why user trust is falling, the points flagged, and suggested fixes
Prepared 29 Sep 2026 · QA Engineering, MUST Company
==============================================================================

Based on: docs/sut-coin-research-2026-09-29.txt (v2) and its sources, and
          docs/sut-top100-validation-plan-2026-09-29.txt

Legal items are REPORTED allegations from the media, not findings. Severity
reflects how much each issue damages trust; it is not a legal judgement.
Not financial or legal advice.

Severity key:  [CRITICAL]  [HIGH]  [MEDIUM]  [POSITIVE]


1. SIGNS THAT TRUST IS FALLING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Price down 97% from the Sep 2025 peak ($14.10-$14.78 -> ~$0.40), with
    three sharp crashes and no explanation from the project.
  - Exchanges dropping it: GOPAX delisted SUT (2025); MEXC no longer trades
    it.
  - Very little trading: ~$70K a day, about 0.1% of the ~$75M market cap.
  - A "MSQUARE victims' chat room" existed, meaning some holders see
    themselves as victims.


2. FLAGGED POINTS AND FIXES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. LEGAL, BANKING AND REGULATORY

 #1 [CRITICAL] Reported criminal complaint
    Finding: A reporter filed a complaint alleging fraud and illegal
             fund-raising (유사수신) against MSQUARE GLOBAL and affiliates
             including SuperTrust, over the "SuperSave" scheme. It reportedly
             involves 30,000+ investors. Unproven; the outcome is unknown.
    Fix:     Publish a clear, lawyer-reviewed statement and give regular
             updates. Pause any product that promises returns until it has
             regulatory clearance.

 #2 [CRITICAL] Bank account freeze
    Finding: Shinhan Bank froze SuperTrust's deposits (Apr 2025) after
             ~KRW 200B in transactions, treating it as an unregistered
             virtual-asset business. A Seoul court upheld the freeze
             (30 Apr 2025); the company appealed.
    Fix:     Register properly as a virtual-asset business (VASP) in Korea,
             or stop the activity that needs registration. Tell users what
             is being done.

 #3 [CRITICAL] Exchange delisting (GOPAX)
    Finding: GOPAX ended support for SUT, reportedly citing possible legal
             violations and failure to disclose material matters.
    Fix:     Fix the disclosure gaps the exchange raised, and publish
             material information on a regular schedule.

 #4 [HIGH] Lawsuits against critics
    Finding: Defamation action against the operator of the victims' chat
             room (sent to prosecutors on 12 Nov 2025), plus a ~KRW 2B
             damages claim.
    Fix:     Answer complaints openly with a public FAQ and a support /
             refund process, instead of suing. Lawsuits against users look
             like silencing.

B. MONEY AND TOKEN PRACTICES

 #5 [CRITICAL] Returns-style and price-support offers
    Finding: - A buyback at KRW 18,000 (KRW 10B total).
             - "1,000 SUT -> 2,000 USDT", only for people who join SuperSave.
             - A published plan for a "short-term SUT value surge".
    Fix:     Stop offers that look like guaranteed returns or price
             support. Any buyback should be unconditional, announced in
             advance, and funded transparently.

 #6 [HIGH] Supply claims contradict each other
    Finding: - "Zero reserve / 100% private sale" is claimed, but GOPAX
               found the foundation held 47.8% (Dec 2024).
             - Three different "circulating" figures are published:
               188.4M, 46.6M and 2.02M.
    Fix:     Publish one wallet map (team, treasury, lock-up addresses and
             balances). Report one circulating figure everywhere.

 #7 [HIGH] Concentrated holdings
    Finding: The top 10 wallets hold ~71% (~50% excluding the dead
             address). One unlabelled wallet holds 21% (50M SUT, received
             21 Oct 2025).
    Fix:     Label the large wallets publicly, and put team and treasury
             tokens under an on-chain time lock that anyone can verify.

 #8 [HIGH] Liquidity not locked
    Finding: About 97% of the main Uniswap pool (~$91K) is controlled by 4
             wallets, and none of it is locked.
    Fix:     Lock the liquidity in a public lock contract, or disclose who
             provides it.

 #9 [MEDIUM] "Burn" didn't reduce total supply
    Finding: 50M SUT was sent to the dead address (Mar 2024), but the
             official total supply still reads 238,403,732.
    Fix:     Explain this clearly, or use a real burn so the total supply
             falls.

 #10 [MEDIUM] Regulatory relabelling
    Finding: The KWT coin was relabelled from stablecoin to utility coin to
             avoid stablecoin rules.
    Fix:     Stop relabelling products to get around regulation. It reads as
             evasion.

C. MARKET BEHAVIOUR

 #11 [CRITICAL] Unexplained crashes
    Finding: -75% (Sep 2025); down to ~$0.44 (Dec 2025); -80% in two days
             (May 2026).
    Fix:     After any large price move, publish what happened, including
             any team or treasury wallet movements. Silence feeds rumours.

 #12 [HIGH] Suspicious trading volume
    Finding: $47M-$73M a day in late 2025 against a ~$3M market cap. This
             pattern is often associated with fake trading (wash trading).
    Fix:     Work only with exchanges that monitor their markets. Publish
             any market-maker agreements.

 #13 [HIGH] Rank from an accounting change
    Finding: #2,049 -> #299 overnight on 10 Feb 2026, when the counted
             circulating supply changed from 2.02M to 188.4M. It was not
             driven by price or adoption.
    Fix:     Don't promote the rank as an achievement. Informed users can see
             how it happened.

 #14 [MEDIUM] Thin market
    Finding: The ±2% order book is only ~$4K-$12K deep, so it is hard to
             exit a position.
    Fix:     Grow real depth through proper, disclosed listings and market
             making, and through real payment use.

D. TRANSPARENCY AND COMMUNICATION

 #15 [HIGH] MSQUARE relationship denied against the evidence
    Finding: SuperTrust says it and MSQUARE GLOBAL are "separate businesses"
             (8 Aug 2026). But news reports call it an MSQUARE affiliate, the
             two filed lawsuits together, and the site code uses msquare_
             labels.
    Fix:     State the corporate relationship accurately. A denial that the
             evidence contradicts damages trust more than the relationship.

 #16 [HIGH] Advisors can't be verified
    Finding: The claimed TON Network and Outlier Ventures advisors can't be
             found anywhere, including the whitepaper and the project site.
    Fix:     Remove unverifiable claims, or publish confirmation from the
             advisors themselves.

 #17 [MEDIUM] No audit, no team identity check (KYC), no bug bounty
    Finding: CertiK lists: not audited, no third-party audit, no KYC, no bug
             bounty; holder concentration "High".
    Fix:     Commission an independent smart-contract audit and a team KYC,
             and publish both. Start a bug bounty.

 #18 [MEDIUM] Messy public data
    Finding: - Trackers disagree on the all-time high ($14.10-$14.78, with
               outliers), on supply and on rank.
             - CoinMarketCap's AI pages mixed SUT up with Sanity United,
               another token that uses the same ticker.
    Fix:     Keep the project's CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko profiles correct
             and up to date, and ask them to fix the mix-up.

 #19 [POSITIVE] Under-communicated strength
    Finding: Contract ownership was renounced (20 Jul 2026). The code is
             verified, cannot mint, has no fees or blacklist, and can no
             longer be paused.
    Fix:     Tell users, with the transaction link. It is a real trust point
             that most people don't know about.

E. PRODUCT AND UTILITY

 #20 [HIGH] Most platforms are unproven
    Finding: - ZERO PLUS app: 50+ downloads.
             - AI Studio 48 and SUT GOLD Connect: no product found.
             - L2U and NATURUBOOK: run by other companies.
             - The SuperTrust app is real (10K+ downloads).
    Fix:     Focus on one or two platforms that actually work, publish real
             usage numbers, and drop features that exist only as
             announcements.

 #21 [MEDIUM] Narrow benefit
    Finding: The 30-50% fee discount, SUT's main selling point, applies only
             to South Korean users of the operator's own platforms.
    Fix:     Widen where SUT can be spent, and publish real payment volume and
             merchant counts.

 #22 [MEDIUM] "RWA" label without backing
    Finding: SUT is described as a real-world-asset (RWA) project, but the
             gold and Mongolian mine claims are unverified. The documentation
             describes a payment and discount token.
    Fix:     Back the label with audited assets and regular
             proof-of-reserve reports, or stop using it.

4. ROOT CAUSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trust is falling mainly because of legal and financial practices, and gaps
between what the project says and what can be verified. The technology is
not the problem; the contract itself is now fairly clean.

Biggest levers, in order:
  1. Compliance: register properly, and stop returns-style offers.
  2. Disclosure: publish the wallet map, one supply figure, and an
     explanation of each crash.
  3. Honesty: state the MSQUARE relationship and the advisors accurately.
  4. Proof: an audit, a team KYC, locked liquidity, and real usage numbers.
